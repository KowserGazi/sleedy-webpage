Privacy Policy

Your privacy, in plain words.

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This page explains what Sleedy collects, why, and the choices you have. We wrote it in plain words on purpose.

The short version

  • On this website we only ask for your email if you join the waitlist.
  • In the app we keep what is needed to run your groups and expenses.
  • We never sell your data and we never use it for ads.
  • You can ask us to delete your data any time at hello@sleedy.app.

1. Who we are

Sleedy is a group expense splitting app and website (sleedy.app), run by Kowser Gazi from Bangladesh. In this page, "we" and "us" mean Sleedy. For any privacy question, write to hello@sleedy.app.

2. What we collect

On the website (the waitlist)

  • The email address you type in, and the date you joined.
  • Our website host may keep basic technical logs, like your IP address and browser type, to keep the site running and secure.

In the Sleedy app (once it launches)

  • Account details for people who create groups: email address, display name, and a password or Google sign-in.
  • People who join a group with a code only give a display name. No account is needed.
  • Group content: group names, member names, expenses, amounts, categories, notes, payments, and any receipt or proof photos you choose to add.
  • Basic device and app information, so we can fix bugs and keep accounts safe.
  • Subscription status. Payments are handled by Google Play, so we never see your card details.

The app may ask for camera access to scan an invite QR code or add a receipt photo. We do not ask for your contacts or your precise location.

3. How we use it

  • To run the waitlist and tell you when Sleedy launches.
  • To run the app: accounts, groups, balances, reports, and sign-in.
  • To keep things secure and to find and fix problems.
  • To reply when you write to us.
  • To follow the law when we have to.

We do not use your data for advertising or to build profiles about you.

4. Why we are allowed to use it

  • Your consent, for the waitlist. You can take it back any time.
  • To give you the service you asked for, like your account and groups.
  • Our legitimate interest in keeping Sleedy safe and working.
  • Legal duties, when the law requires something from us.

5. Who can see your data

  • Other members of your group can see the group's content, like expenses and who paid.
  • Service providers that help us run Sleedy, such as Google (sign-in, spreadsheets, Play billing), email delivery, and cloud hosting and storage. They only get what they need to do their job.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.

We never sell personal data and we never share it for advertising.

6. Where your data is kept

We are based in Bangladesh. Our providers may store data in other countries. We choose providers with strong security, and where the law requires it we use proper safeguards for transfers.

7. How long we keep it

  • Waitlist emails: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. We will clear the waitlist after launch.
  • Account and group data: while your account is open. When you ask us to delete it, we remove it within 30 days. Copies in backups may remain a little longer before they are overwritten.

8. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you your data, correct it, delete it, or give you a copy. You can also object to how we use it, or take back your consent. Every email we send will have an unsubscribe link.

To use any of these rights, write to hello@sleedy.app. We will reply within 30 days. If you live in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority. If you live in California, you have the rights to know, delete and correct your data, and we do not sell it.

9. Cookies and tracking

Right now this website does not use tracking cookies, ads, or analytics. If that changes, we will update this page and ask you first where the law says we must.

10. Security

We use secure (HTTPS) connections and limit who can reach your data. No system is perfect, so if you think something is wrong with your account, tell us right away.

11. Children

Sleedy is not made for children under 13 (or under 16 where local law says so). We do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this page

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. For big changes, we will also email waitlist members and app users.
